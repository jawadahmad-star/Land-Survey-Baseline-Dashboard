Land Dispute Vignette — Ayesha & Amir

Respondents were read a short scenario about inheritance and asked to judge it. The vignette measures attitudes toward a woman exercising her inheritance rights, and perceptions of how local institutions (the patwari and the courts) would adjudicate the dispute.

📖 The scenario: Ayesha and Amir are sister and brother. After their father's death, both inherited a share of his agricultural land. Ayesha wants to sell her share; her brother Amir refuses to allow the sale and continues farming the land. Respondents were asked how justified each sibling is, and — if Ayesha approached the patwari (land registrar) or the courts — who they believe each institution would side with.

Mouza Completion Tracker

Live completion status for every mouza in the sampling frame — completed interviews (urban / rural) plus the assigned IDs still remaining. A mouza is Completed when both urban and rural are done, where each side is done once it either meets its completed target or has run out of IDs (every assigned ID touched — only refusals left, none to replace them). It stays In Progress while either side still has untouched IDs and an unmet target. Search by mouza or tehsil, filter by status, and click any column to sort.

Mouza Tehsil Urban Completed Rural Completed Total Completed Remaining IDs Completion ▼ Status

Urban = respondents tracked in the city; Rural = respondents tracked in the village (per the PULSE sampling tracker). Remaining IDs (🏙️ urban · 🌾 rural) = assigned IDs in the sampling pool not yet touched by the field team — an ID is "touched" at any final outcome (completed, refused, locked, respondent not found, etc.). Status: a mouza is Completed when both urban and rural are done — each side is done once it hits its completed target or its remaining IDs reach 0 (all IDs touched, only refusals left). Completion % = total completed interviews ÷ mouza target. Sorted by activity/completion by default.

Intervention Completion Tracker

Progress of the women's land-rights intervention. Only households whose baseline interview was completed in a revealed treatment mouza (T1 / T2) are eligible — Control mouzas and mouzas whose arm is not yet revealed are excluded. All progress here is measured against that eligible pool — overall, by treatment arm, and mouza-by-mouza. Scope expands automatically as more arms are revealed.
